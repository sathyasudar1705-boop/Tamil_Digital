Frequently Asked Questions

Can I reschedule my session?

Yes! You can reschedule your booking free of charge up to 24 hours before your session directly from your customer dashboard.

What is the turnaround time for retouched soft copies?

Typically, raw images are delivered via online secure link within 48 hours. The finalized retouched soft copies take 5 to 7 business days depending on the selected package.

Do you support outdoor sessions?

Our Premium and Professional packages include outdoor locations within city limits. Basic is limited to indoor setups in our main studio room.
